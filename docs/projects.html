Projects

Projects and tools I work on, in biodiversity monitoring and species conservation.

Current projects

BiodivPond pilot

A transnational Biodiversa+ pilot building a European pond monitoring network. I coordinate the pilot, which combines eDNA, passive acoustic monitoring and field surveys with a citizen-science scheme across hundreds of ponds.

Biodiversa+

The Horizon Europe partnership on biodiversity, bringing together 75 organisations from 37 countries. I contribute to the biodiversity monitoring work stream, on priority setting for monitoring and on the pilots that test novel methods.

PROSPECTIVE LIFE

A ten-year integrated LIFE project on active species protection in Czechia, for taxa whose favourable conservation status cannot be reached by habitat management alone. I work on the condition evaluation framework underpinning it.

ndopred

An R package that turns occurrence records from NDOP, the Czech nature conservation species occurrence database, into IUCN Red List metrics and categories for national and regional Red List work. Ships two Shiny dashboards.

host_naturecz

The codebase behind a system for evaluating the condition of species and habitats in Czechia across scales, from individual sites to the national Natura 2000 reporting picture.

Past projects

ConNaturLIFE Ukraine

A preparatory LIFE project transferring Czech nature conservation expertise, including citizen science approaches, to conservation authorities in Ukraine. It supported Ukraine’s first nationwide bioblitz.

LIFE-IP N2K Revisited

The first integrated LIFE project in Czechia, known domestically as Jedna příroda, setting up a more effective system of care for Natura 2000 sites. My work sat in the monitoring and evaluation work package.
